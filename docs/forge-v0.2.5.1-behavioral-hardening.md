## Frozen research question

> Can FinSight Bench distinguish agents that merely follow obvious decision
> thresholds from agents that reason about uncertainty, conflicting evidence,
> trust boundaries and experiment value?

This milestone preserves v0.2.5 as an immutable baseline. Its contracts, tool
plane, agent loop, verifier, API adapter, suite, trajectories, and released
artifact are not edited. v0.2.5.1 is a new package, suite, runner, checkpoint,
and artifact lineage.

## Selectable experiment contract

Each public task exposes a metric-free experiment catalog containing tool,
stage, engine, minimum certification, capability scope, prerequisites, logical
experiment cost, expected experiment wall time, and high-fidelity status.

Metrics, availability, future evidence, expected verdict, task class, paired-case
identity, required gates, and oracle path remain sealed. Experiments may be chosen
in any dependency-valid order. Trust is checked before sealed evidence is read.
Unavailable and refused stages expose no metrics and consume no engine run,
high-fidelity run, or logical experiment cost.

The four verdicts are `ACCEPT`, `REJECT`, `ABSTAIN`, and `NOT_COMPARABLE`.
`NOT_COMPARABLE` is valid only after observed evidence establishes that two engine
results lie outside their common capability intersection; it is not a synonym
for missing or untrusted evidence.

## Experiment Selection Regret

Every sealed task defines one oracle-minimal valid action path and its frozen
resource vector. Regret is graded only when the submitted verdict is correct,
the required evidence gates are present, and the run completes. Invalid or
under-researched conclusions have no regret value; they receive no artificial
credit for spending less than a valid oracle path.

```text
USD regret           = experiment_cost_agent - experiment_cost_oracle
Tool-call regret     = tool_calls_agent - tool_calls_oracle
High-fidelity regret = high_fidelity_runs_agent - high_fidelity_runs_oracle
Wall-time regret     = experiment_wall_agent - experiment_wall_oracle
```

Logical experiment cost and wall time are frozen research-resource estimates,
distinct from API inference cost and measured provider latency. The artifact
exposes regret coverage, zero-regret rate, and the mean of each component.
Correctness does not disappear when an agent over-researches: a correct,
evidence-complete path can be a Verified Research Success and still carry regret.
This separation supplies clean routing targets for v0.4.

## Frozen live result

The 72-episode matrix completed on 2026-09-12. The independently verified
artifact is frozen at
[`data/exports/forge_v0_2_5_1_behavioral_hardening`](../data/exports/forge_v0_2_5_1_behavioral_hardening)

All 72 episodes and all six model/seed tiers were admitted, the source manifest

The hardened distribution exposes consistent behavioral weaknesses that the
easier baseline could not isolate:

- all three tiers rejected the tiny-sample case instead of abstaining in both
  seeds;
- all three tiers abstained on the mediocre-headline but strongly robust case
  instead of accepting it, and omitted required evidence;
- all three tiers submitted `NOT_COMPARABLE` for the out-of-scope engine
  disagreement, but none gathered the evidence required to justify that verdict;
- the near-two-Sharpe pair was cleanly separated: every tier rejected case 01
  after its survival failure and accepted case 09 after the selected latency test;
- Sol had one seed-sensitive miss on the narrow threshold case, while Luna had
  one seed-sensitive miss on the cheap decisive screen.

Experiment Selection Regret was defined for the 52 verified paths (72.22%
coverage). Mean tool-call regret was `0.980769`; mean logical USD,
high-fidelity-run, and wall-time regret were all zero. Only one eligible path
was exactly oracle-minimal, giving a 1.92% zero-regret rate. The result therefore
separates two facts: most successful conclusions used the right experiments,
but almost every successful trajectory made one avoidable tool call.

These figures are diagnostic measurements over two seeds, not a statistical
leaderboard. Terra's one-episode lead is reported descriptively and is not a
claim of tier superiority.
